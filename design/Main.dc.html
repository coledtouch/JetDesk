Where the money is
The spread between two FBOs is bigger than the spread between two airlines.

Jet A at the FBO the owner likes can run two dollars a gallon over the field twenty minutes away. On a 150-gallon load that is $345, before you count what the extra landing costs you. JetDesk counts it. Every stop is scored against its own burn, detour and ramp fee, and the answer is a number with a sign in front of it.

Straight answers
The three things every pilot asks first.
Does it replace ForeFlight?
No. It is the desk beside it. You still file and fly in ForeFlight; JetDesk is where the cost, runway and FBO homework gets done first, and every trip opens in ForeFlight with one tap.
Does it work with no signal?
Yes. The airport and runway database, your trips, prices, notes and both calculators live on the phone. Weather, winds and market data rejoin the moment you have coverage.
Where do fuel prices come from?
From you and your crew, in ten seconds per airport, with AirNav one tap away to check. Prices flag themselves stale after two weeks. The market reference (EIA spot and live crude) tells you when the whole board is moving.
