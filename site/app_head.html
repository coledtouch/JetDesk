How it works

01

Build the trip

Add legs by airport code. Distance, winds-corrected time, burn and cost fill in per leg with the arrival runway graded. One tap opens the route in ForeFlight.
02

Check the field

Live METAR and TAF, crosswind per runway, density altitude, fuel and repair on the FAA file, your crew's prices and FBO notes, and alternates within 25, 40 or 60 nm.
03

Score the fuel stop

Pick where you land and where the cheap gas is. The calculator charges the stop against you and hands back a signed dollar figure and a breakeven.

Straight answers

Does it replace ForeFlight?
No. It is the desk beside it. You still file and fly in ForeFlight; JetDesk is where the cost, runway and FBO homework gets done first, and every trip opens in ForeFlight with one tap.
Does it work with no signal?
Yes. The airport and runway database, your trips, prices, notes and both calculators live on the phone. Weather, winds and market data rejoin the moment you have coverage.
Where do fuel prices come from?
From you and your crew, in ten seconds per airport, with AirNav one tap away to check. Prices flag themselves stale after two weeks. The market reference tells you when the whole board is moving.
What happens after the 14-day trial?
Your account continues on Free. Nothing is charged because no card was taken, and your trips, prices and notes stay. Subscribe to Pro whenever the math says it pays for itself.
Can I try it without an account?
Yes. Explore airport and runway data first. A free account adds saved trips, your price log and the 14-day Pro trial with no credit card.
